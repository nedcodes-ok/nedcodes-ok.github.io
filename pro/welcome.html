You're in. Here's how to get started.

Your Pro license key is in the confirmation email from Gumroad. Three commands and you're done.

1

Activate your license

Run this from your project root. Paste the key from your Gumroad receipt.

npx cursor-doctor activate YOUR-KEY-HERE
2

See what's wrong

Get the full picture before fixing anything. The audit shows conflicts, redundancy, token waste, and everything scan doesn't cover.

npx cursor-doctor audit
3

Fix it

Preview first, then apply. 34 auto-fixers handle frontmatter, globs, duplicates, formatting, and more.

npx cursor-doctor fix --dry-run
npx cursor-doctor fix

VS Code / Cursor users: The extension picks up your Pro license automatically. You'll see quick-fix lightbulbs and the Fix All command in the palette. No extra setup needed.
